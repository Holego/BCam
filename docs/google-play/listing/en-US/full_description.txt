Background Camera records video without you having to keep the app open. Set it up once, add the widget to your home screen, and from then on one tap starts a recording and one tap stops it. Recording carries on with the screen locked.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose a folder, a camera and a quality once.
• Add the Background Camera widget to your home screen.
• Tap it to start recording. Tap it again, or press Stop in the notification, to finish.

A notification is always shown while a recording is running, and Android shows its own camera and microphone indicators. The app never hides what it is doing.

FEATURES
• One-tap home-screen widget
• Stop button in the ongoing notification
• Keeps recording with the screen off and after the app is closed from recent apps
• Videos are saved straight into a folder you choose; nothing is uploaded anywhere
• Choose the camera, resolution, frame rate and file size (Standard, Light, Smallest)
• Optional audio recording
• English and Russian, switchable inside the app

YOUR VIDEOS STAY WITH YOU
Recordings and settings stay on your device. The app has no account and no analytics of its own.

FREE, WITH ADS YOU CAN TURN OFF
The app is free and shows ads on its setup screen, never while you are recording and never when you use the widget. An optional auto-renewing subscription turns all ads off while it is active; the price and renewal period are shown in the app and in Google Play before you subscribe, and you can cancel at any time in Google Play. You can also watch a short video to turn ads off for 24 hours.

PERMISSIONS
• Camera: to record video
• Microphone: only if you switch on audio recording
• Notifications: to show that a recording is running
• Foreground service: keeps the recording alive while the screen is off

Please respect other people's privacy. Recording people may require their consent and is regulated by law in many countries; you are responsible for using the app lawfully.
